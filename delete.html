Thingrun · Privacy Policy

Delete your Thingrun data

Developer: Okan Sayar (Gamon Studio)

In the game (instant)

  1. Open Thingrun and tap Settings (gear icon).
  2. Tap Privacy, then Delete my data, and tap again to confirm.

This deletes your game account (Unity Authentication), your cloud save, your leaderboard entries and the objects stored on your device, and requests deletion of your analytics data.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Send an email to osayar.thingrun@gmail.com with the subject "Thingrun data deletion". Include your Player ID if you have it (shown at the bottom of Settings). We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Photos are never stored, so there is nothing to delete. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law; we keep no payment data.

Thingrun verilerini sil

Oyunda: Ayarlar › Gizlilik › Verilerimi sil, onaylamak için bir kez daha dokun. Oyun hesabın, bulut kaydın, lig kayıtların ve cihazdaki objeler silinir; analitik verilerinin silinmesi istenir.

E-postayla: osayar.thingrun@gmail.com adresine "Thingrun veri silme" konulu e-posta gönder; varsa Ayarlar'daki oyuncu numaranı ekle. 30 gün içinde silinir ve e-postayla bildirilir.
